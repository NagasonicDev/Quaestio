# Quaestio

**A calm, flexible place to build a question bank, practise what you know, and make your own test papers.**

Quaestio works in your browser and keeps your courses and study history on your device. Use it with a sample course to get a feel for the app, or shape a question bank around the way you study.

## Study your way

- **Build a course that fits.** Organise questions into topics and subtopics, then browse by subject, difficulty, or question type.
- **Practise with a purpose.** Start a focused session, mix things up, or work through questions due for review. Rate how each question went to shape your next review.
- **See your progress.** Check your recent activity and what is coming up, then pick up where you left off.
- **Make a timed quiz.** Put together a session from the questions in your bank and practise under time pressure.
- **Create printable tests.** Choose questions and generate a paper as a PDF or Word document, with a preview in the app.
- **Write rich questions.** Add formatted text, equations, images, tables, lists, and multi-part prompts. Keep answers and marking criteria with each question.
- **Bring your questions with you.** Import questions from JSON, or export and restore a complete course—including its images—as a `.qb` file.
- **Use it comfortably.** Dark mode and a responsive layout make it easy to study across screen sizes.

## Get started

### Try it online

Open the **Quaestio GitHub Pages site**: [Launch Quaestio](https://nagasonicdev.github.io/quaestio/)

Your courses are saved in the browser you use to open it. They are not synced between devices or browser profiles, so export a course backup if you want to move it or keep a copy.

### Run it on your computer

Install [Node.js 20.19 or later](https://nodejs.org/) (or Node.js 22.12 or later), download or clone this repository, then:

1. Open the project folder.
2. Double-click `Start Quaestio.bat` on Windows, `Start Quaestio.command` on macOS, or run `./start.sh` on Linux.
3. Keep the terminal window open while you use Quaestio. The launcher opens the app in your browser.

The first launch installs the app's dependencies. After that, start it again with the same launcher whenever you want to study.

## Your courses and privacy

Quaestio stores course content, images, practice history, review schedules, and generated tests in browser storage on your device. The app does not use a Quaestio account or sync your data to a Quaestio server.

That means each browser profile has its own copy. Use **Export course** to download a `.qb` backup, then import that file in another browser or keep it somewhere safe. Clearing the browser's site data can erase your local courses, so back up anything you want to keep.
