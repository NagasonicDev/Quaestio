Sample answer: 
Crediting sources of data is important as it allows the researcher to show evidence of their 
results and research findings. A researcher can credit data sources through the construction 
of a bibliography which provides references of the secondary research articles, while 
maintaining integrity and preventing plagiarism. An appendix can include articles and data 
sources used in the research process to enhance authenticity.

Sample answer: 
The democratic parenting style provides the best opportunity to build a positive relationship 
with a dependant because this style encourages open communication, allowing dependants 
to express themselves and also builds trust between the dependant and the parent. When

Sample answer: 
Both foster and stepparents are legally unable to make major health and medical decisions 
on behalf of a dependant, for example, electing to receive vaccinations. The legal constraints 
can affect the social parents’ decision-making authority, potentially causing stress and 
frustration, affecting their emotional wellbeing. The stepparent may have a deep emotional 
connection, therefore, not being able to make major medical or health decisions may leave 
them feeling frustrated and helpless. Having this additional complication can affect their 
emotional wellbeing as they may feel ‘less’ of a parent if they are unable to make these 
important decisions. Foster parents often have limited information on a child’s medical 
history. They can experience conflict with biological parents in relation to health and medical 
decisions, this conflict can cause stress, affecting their physical wellbeing.

Sample answer: 
A researcher should carefully select a sample representing a subset of the population to 
ensure valid and reliable data is collected. For example, to ensure validity, when conducting 
research about primary school aged children in NSW, it would be essential to ensure that the 
students researched are of primary school age. A r esearcher should consider their sample 
size to ensure the total number of participants will accurately represent the total population. 
A large sample size ensures reliability so if the research was repeated it would provide 
similar results. A r esearcher could apply a stratified random sam
 
ple method to allow t he 
researcher to organise their sample population into categories eg city, regional and remote. 
The researcher would then select a similar number of individuals to research across the 
different categories, further enhancing reliability and validity of the research.

Sample answer: 
The Family Law Act 1975 was created to protect the welfare of children whose parents 
separate or divorce and provides a process to make decisions about who the child spends 
time with and who is responsible for making decisions. This legislation is effective in 
protecting the welfare of children as the intent of the law is to focus on their needs. It is also 
effective as there are processes to follow in relation to children at risk, however, most 
families that engage in this process have a long and drawn-out experience. This impacts on 
the welfare of the child as decisions are not usually processed quickly, creating instability 
and stress for the child.

Workplace culture initiatives

immediate threats to safety. The laws increase worker efficiency as workers are confident 
that their safety needs are upheld and are more likely to work efficiently without fear of 
injury, disability or death.
