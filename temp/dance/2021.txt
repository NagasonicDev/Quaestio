– 2 –
Total marks – 40
Attempt Questions 1–3
Allow about 20 minutes for Question 1
Allow about 20 minutes for Question 2
Allow about 35 minutes for Question 3
Answer each question in a SEPARATE writing booklet. Extra writing booklets are available.
Your answers will be assessed on how well you:
● demonstrate dance knowledge and understanding relevant to the question
● communicate ideas and information using relevant examples and terminology
● present a logical and cohesive response
 
Question 1 (10 marks)
How have Pina Bausch’s choreographic practices contributed to her being recognised as a 
seminal artist?
In your response, provide specific examples from her works.
OR
How have Ohad Naharin’s choreographic practices contributed to him being recognised as a 
seminal artist?
In your response, provide specific examples from his works.
Question 2 (10 marks)
Twyla Tharp reinvented dance to reflect a changing world. 
Explain this statement in relation to the works of Twyla Tharp from the 1960s–1980s.
In your response, provide specific examples from her works.
OR
Akram Khan reinvented dance to reflect a changing world. 
Explain this statement in relation to the works of Akram Khan from the 1990s–present.
In your response, provide specific examples from his works.
Question 3 (20 marks)
Explain how Mats Ek uses dance and theatrical elements to explore the emotions of everyday 
situations.
In your response, provide specific examples from Appartement.
