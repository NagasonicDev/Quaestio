Total marks – 40 
Attempt Questions 1–3 
Allow about 20 minutes for Question 1 
Allow about 20 minutes for Question 2 
Allow about 35 minutes for Question 3 
Answer each question in a SEPARATE writing booklet. Extra writing booklets are available. 
Your answers will be assessed on how well you: 
●  demonstrate dance knowledge and understanding relevant to the question 
●  communicate ideas and information using relevant examples and terminology 
●  present a logical and cohesive response 
Question 1 (10 marks)  
Explain how Pina Bausch has developed dance as an artform.  
In your response, provide specific examples from her works.  
OR 
Explain how Ohad Naharin has developed dance as an artform. 
In your response, provide specific examples from his works. 
Question 2 (10 marks) 
Explain the relationship between Twyla Tharp’s work and the major social, cultural, political 
and/or historical events of the 1960s–1980s. 
In your response, provide specific examples from her works. 
OR 
Explain the relationship between Akram Khan’s work and the major social, cultural, political 
and/or historical events of the 1990s–present. 
In your response, provide specific examples from his works. 
Question 3 (20 marks) 
Evaluate how Mats Ek uses his distinctive choreographic style to communicate the central 
ideas of Appartement. 
In your response, provide specific examples from the work.
