1
Sample answer:
There are 7! possible seating arrangements at a round table without restriction.
There are  6! × 2  possible seating arrangements where the two people sit together.
Hence, there are  7! − 6! × 2 = 3600  seating arrangements where they do not sit together.

Question 14 (a)
Criteria
Marks
• Provides correct proof
2
• Creates 6 appropriate buckets, or equivalent merit
1
Sample answer:
Create 6 buckets 61, 60@, 62, 30@, 63, 20@, 64, 15@, 65, 12@, 66, 10@.
If we choose 7 numbers, then by the pigeonhole principle two numbers must be chosen from 
the same bucket. These two numbers multiply to 60. Hence, the product of all 7 numbers is a 
multiple of 60.
